- **Refunds.** If a wallet route fails, the transaction reverts and no tokens move, but the gas you paid is spent. If a deposit-address route cannot complete, the provider sends your deposit back to the refund address, minus any provider and network fees.
- **No partial fills.** L2Pass does not use partial execution. A swap completes in full or does not execute.
- **No KYC, same screening for every swap.** You need no account and no KYC. On L2Pass RFQ and L2Pass Private, a flagged address gets no quote, so nothing is sent. A deposit flagged after it arrives is held for review instead of refunded; you resolve it with NEAR Intents support. See [Failed swaps and refunds](/products/swap/learn/failed-swaps-and-refunds#how-screening-works).
