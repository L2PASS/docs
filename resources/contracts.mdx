## Security Reminder

- **No Direct Transfers:** L2Pass will never ask you to send funds directly to contract addresses. The only address you send funds to is the one-time deposit address shown in the swap interface for your quote. Always use our official interface: [L2Pass Swap](https://swap.l2pass.com/).
- **Anti-Phishing:** Be vigilant against look-alike addresses; always cross-reference the full contract address.
