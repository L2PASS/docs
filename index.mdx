## Overview

The global yield landscape is inefficient.

More than **\$17T** sits in traditional savings accounts earning **under 2% APY**, while inflation erodes real returns and banks capture most of the spread.

At the same time, on chain markets such as lending protocols and tokenized treasury products often generate **5 to 10× higher yield**, operating **24/7 with transparent execution**.

Yet this capital remains fragmented. Over **\$150B in stablecoins** is spread across multiple blockchains such as Tron, Ethereum, and L2 networks, making liquidity difficult to move and deploy efficiently.

L2Pass connects these fragmented liquidity pools and provides infrastructure for moving stablecoins across chains and deploying them into yield strategies.

---

### The Gap

Despite the opportunity, access remains limited.

**For users**\
Only about **2% interact with DeFi directly**, while many avoid it due to complexity, security concerns, and multi step processes involving bridges, swaps, and gas management.

**For financial applications**\
Fintechs, wallets, and payment platforms lack simple infrastructure for cross chain settlement and yield integration. Supporting multiple networks often requires complex custom systems.

As a result, large amounts of stablecoin capital remain idle or locked inside single ecosystems.

---

### Closing the Gap

L2Pass provides the infrastructure needed to make stablecoin liquidity portable and productive.

It enables stablecoins to move across networks, aggregates liquidity from multiple chains, and connects capital to on chain yield strategies through a unified execution layer.

> The result is a more efficient system where stablecoin liquidity can **move freely and generate yield across the crypto economy**.
