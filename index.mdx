L2Pass moves your tokens between blockchains in one step: USDT from Tron to Ethereum, BTC to USDT, USDC to Solana. It works across **14 networks**, including Bitcoin, Zcash, Tron, Solana and TON, and you can swap publicly or privately.

For every swap, L2Pass asks several routes for a quote and shows you what each one pays out and charges before you confirm. L2Pass never holds your funds. Stablecoin swaps carry a **0% L2Pass fee**. Wallets and apps get the same routing through the **L2Pass Swap API**.

## How It Works

For every quote, L2Pass asks several independent routes at once and sorts them by what you receive:

- **L2Pass RFQ and L2Pass Private by NEAR Intents:** solvers and market makers compete to fill your swap.
- **Native bridges:** USDT0 over LayerZero and Circle CCTP for USDC.
- **Aggregators and intent networks:** LI.FI, Relay, Aori and the OKX DEX aggregator.

You see the amount you receive, the minimum received, the estimated time and the fees for each route before you confirm. The amount shown is after fees. Only source-chain gas and any bridge message fee are paid separately. [How routes are compared →](/products/swap/learn/compare-routes)

## Why L2Pass

- **Several quotes, not one.** Every swap is quoted by NEAR Intents solvers, native bridges and aggregators at once, and sorted by what you receive.
- **Every chain, not only EVM.** Bitcoin, Zcash, Tron, Solana and TON sit next to Ethereum and its L2s.
- **Privacy when you need it.** Private swaps on the same interface, with the same compliance rules.
- **Non-custodial.** L2Pass never holds your tokens or keys. No account and no KYC for regular use.
- **Transparent fees.** 0% on stablecoin swaps, up to 0.2% on other assets, up to 0.3% on private swaps. [Fees explained →](/products/swap/learn/fees)
