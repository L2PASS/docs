## Overview

Stablecoins have become the main way value moves on chain, operating **24/7 with transparent settlement**.

Yet this liquidity is fragmented. Stablecoins are spread across many blockchains such as Tron, Ethereum, Solana and L2 networks, and each bridge, DEX and solver network offers different rates, fees and speeds. Moving value between them is slow to compare and easy to overpay for.

L2Pass connects these fragmented liquidity pools and provides infrastructure for moving stablecoins across chains at the best available rate.

---

### The Gap

Despite the demand, access remains difficult.

**For users**\
Only about **2% interact with DeFi directly**, while many avoid it due to complexity, security concerns, and multi step processes involving bridges, swaps, and gas management.

**For financial applications**\
Fintechs, wallets, and payment platforms lack simple infrastructure for cross chain settlement. Supporting multiple networks often requires complex custom systems.

As a result, large amounts of stablecoin capital remain idle or locked inside single ecosystems.

---

### Closing the Gap

L2Pass provides the infrastructure needed to make stablecoin liquidity portable.

It enables stablecoins to move across networks and aggregates liquidity from multiple bridges, solver networks and liquidity sources through a single interface.

> The result is a more efficient system where stablecoin liquidity can **move freely across the crypto economy**.
