## Overview

L2Pass Swap is a non-custodial interface for swapping tokens across chains, with a focus on stablecoins. It compares quotes from several independent routes at once, including L2Pass RFQ by NEAR Intents, native USDT0 and Circle CCTP bridges, and aggregators, and shows you every option before you confirm.

- **14 networks:** Ethereum, Bitcoin, Zcash, Tron, Solana, TON, BNB Chain, Arbitrum, Base, Polygon, Optimism, Avalanche, Plasma and HyperEVM.
- **0% L2Pass fee on stablecoin swaps.** Up to 0.2% on other assets and up to 0.3% on private swaps. Every fee is already included in the amount shown in the quote.
- **Private swaps:** execute your swap in a confidential environment that limits what public blockchains show.
- **Pay from any wallet or exchange:** send to a one-time deposit address, no wallet connection needed.
- **No account, no KYC for regular use.** L2Pass never holds your tokens or keys.

---

### Why L2Pass

Stablecoin liquidity is spread across many blockchains, such as Tron, Ethereum, Solana and L2 networks. Every bridge, DEX and solver network offers different rates, fees and speeds, so moving value between them is slow to compare and easy to overpay for.

**For users**\
Instead of checking bridges one by one, you see the routes side by side with the amount you will receive, the minimum received and the estimated time.

**For wallets and apps**\
The [L2Pass Swap API](/products/swap/learn/swap-api) adds cross-chain stablecoin swaps to a wallet or app without building custom integrations for every network.

> The result: stablecoins that **move freely across chains at the best available rate**.
