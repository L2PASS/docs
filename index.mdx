## What You Can Do

| You want to | With L2Pass |
| :-- | :-- |
| Move stablecoins between networks | USDT and USDC across Tron, Ethereum, Solana, TON, BNB Chain, L2s and more, with 0% L2Pass fee |
| Swap major assets natively | BTC on Bitcoin, ZEC on Zcash, ETH, SOL and others, to and from stablecoins, without wrapped tokens on your side |
| Keep a swap private | Choose L2Pass Private: the swap runs in a confidential environment that limits what public blockchains show |
| Pay someone on their network | Set their address as the recipient and pay from whatever you hold, in any supported token |
| Swap straight from an exchange | Withdraw to a one-time deposit address, no wallet connection needed |
| Add swaps to your product | Integrate the Swap API, or refer users and share fees |

## How It Works

For every quote, L2Pass asks several independent routes at once and sorts them by what you receive:

- **L2Pass RFQ and L2Pass Private by NEAR Intents:** solvers and market makers compete to fill your swap.
- **Native bridges:** USDT0 over LayerZero and Circle CCTP for USDC.
- **Aggregators and intent networks:** LI.FI, Relay, Aori and the OKX DEX aggregator.

You see the amount you receive, the minimum received, the estimated time and the fees for each route before you confirm. All fees are already included in the quoted amount. [How routes are compared →](/products/swap/learn/compare-routes)

## Why L2Pass

- **Best available rate, not one provider's rate.** Routes compete for every swap.
- **Every chain, not only EVM.** Bitcoin, Zcash, Tron, Solana and TON sit next to Ethereum and its L2s.
- **Privacy when you need it.** Private swaps on the same interface, with the same compliance rules.
- **Non-custodial.** L2Pass never holds your tokens or keys. No account and no KYC for regular use.
- **Transparent fees.** 0% on stablecoin swaps, up to 0.2% on other assets, up to 0.3% on private swaps. [Fees explained →](/products/swap/learn/fees)
