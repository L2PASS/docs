[L2Pass Swap](https://swap.l2pass.com/) was built to solve the fragmentation of cross-chain liquidity, especially for stablecoins. It is a non-custodial interface that compares quotes from several independent routing providers and lets you execute the best one with a single confirmation.

L2Pass never takes custody of your tokens. Swaps are executed by third-party bridges, solver networks and liquidity sources, and the interface shows you each route side by side before you confirm.

## Supported Networks and Assets

L2Pass Swap currently supports **Ethereum, Bitcoin, Zcash, Tron, Solana, TON, BNB Chain, Arbitrum, Base, Polygon, Optimism, Avalanche, Plasma and HyperEVM**.

Stablecoins (USDT, USDT0, USDC, DAI and others) are the main focus, but you can also swap major assets such as ETH, BTC, SOL, ZEC, TRX, BNB, AVAX and POL. Available tokens, networks and routes can change at any time; the [swap interface](https://swap.l2pass.com/) always shows the current list.

## How Routing Works

When you request a quote, L2Pass asks several routing venues at once and shows every route that can fill your swap, sorted by what you will receive:

- **L2Pass RFQ:** Request-for-quote routing through the [NEAR Intents](https://near.org/intents) network, where solvers and market makers compete to fill your swap.
- **L2Pass Private:** The same solver network, executed in a confidential environment. See [Private Swaps](#private-swaps).
- **USDT Native Bridge:** Native USDT0 transfers over LayerZero, including from Tron.
- **USDC Native Bridge:** Circle CCTP V2 transfers of native USDC, in Standard and Fast modes.
- **Aggregators and intent networks:** LI.FI, LI.FI Intents, Relay, Aori and the OKX DEX aggregator.

Each route card shows the expected output, the minimum received, the estimated time and the fees for that route, so you can pick the one that suits you.

## How a Swap Is Executed

Depending on the route, a swap is executed in one of two ways:

- **Wallet transaction:** You sign a transaction in your wallet and the route's contracts execute the swap and bridge on-chain.
- **Deposit address:** For RFQ and Private routes, you send tokens to a one-time deposit address for your quote. You can send from your connected wallet, or transfer manually from any wallet using the address or QR code. The swap then moves through *Awaiting deposit*, *Deposit confirmed* and *Complete*, which you can follow in **Recent swaps**.

For a deposit-address route, you also set a **refund address** on the source chain. If the swap cannot be completed, your deposit is returned there.

## Execution Time

Settlement time depends on the route and the networks involved:

- **Fast routes** (Relay, Aori, RFQ, CCTP Fast) usually settle within seconds to about a minute.
- **Bridge routes** such as USDT0 typically take a few minutes.
- **Slower routes** such as CCTP Standard can take up to about 20 minutes while waiting for block finality.

The estimated time is shown on each route before you confirm. In rare cases with network congestion or provider downtime, a swap can take longer.

## Slippage and Price Impact

**Price impact** is the price shift caused by the trade size relative to the available liquidity. Comparing several venues helps keep it low.

**Slippage** is the difference between the quoted price and the final execution price.

- **Slippage tolerance:** The default tolerance is **0.05%**. You can change it from any route card.
- **Fixed output:** Some routes, such as native USDT0 and USDC bridges, deliver a fixed amount, so slippage does not apply.
- **Minimum received:** Each route shows the **Minimum Received** amount, the worst-case outcome within your tolerance. If the price moves beyond it, the swap does not execute: on-chain routes revert, and deposit-address routes return your deposit to your refund address.

## Fee Structure

The actual fees for your swap are always shown in the interface on the route card before you confirm. The **estimated amount on the destination network already includes all fees**.

### L2Pass Fee

| Swap type | L2Pass fee |
| :-- | :-- |
| Stablecoin swaps via RFQ | **0%** |
| Other assets | **up to 0.2%** |
| Private swaps | **up to 0.3%** |

### Partner and Network Fees

Routing partners (bridges, solver networks, aggregators and liquidity providers) may charge their own fees. L2Pass does not control these fees; for the most up-to-date information, please check each provider's documentation.

Every blockchain transaction also requires a network (gas) fee paid in the native token, such as ETH, POL, BNB or TRX. Gas fees go to the network, not to L2Pass, and are not refundable.

## Private Swaps

Private swaps route your swap through a confidential execution environment instead of a public one, which limits what is visible on public blockchains. They are made possible by NEAR Intents, a third-party protocol.

- **Not anonymity:** Your public wallet address is still included in requests sent to providers.
- **Compliance still applies:** Private swaps are subject to the same transaction screening and sanctions controls as other swaps.
- **Extra fee:** Private swaps carry an L2Pass fee of up to 0.3%, shown in the quote.

See the [Terms of Service](https://l2pass.com/terms-of-service) and [Privacy & AML Principles](https://l2pass.com/privacy-policy) for details.

## Execution Guarantees

- **No partial fills:** L2Pass does not use partial execution. A swap either completes in full or does not execute.
- **Refunds:** If a swap cannot be completed, for example because the price moved beyond your tolerance or the quote expired, your deposit is returned to your refund address. Provider and network fees may be deducted from the refunded amount.
- **Compliance reviews:** If a provider flags a swap as high risk under its AML or sanctions rules, the provider decides whether to complete, refund or hold it, and a refund is not guaranteed. L2Pass cannot override a provider's review.
