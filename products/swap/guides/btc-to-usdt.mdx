You can swap native BTC on Bitcoin to USDT on Tron or Ethereum, and USDT back to BTC, with [L2Pass Swap](https://swap.l2pass.com/). In test quotes, two routes were offered for these pairs, L2Pass RFQ and L2Pass Private, both run on [NEAR Intents](https://near.org/intents). A swap from BTC took about 13.5 to 14 minutes, and a swap to BTC about 9 minutes, mostly waiting for Bitcoin block confirmations. You pay by sending BTC or USDT to a one-time deposit address from any wallet or exchange, with no account. The L2Pass fee is up to 0.2% (up to 0.3% on private swaps), and the quote shows what you will receive after fees. Only source-chain gas and any bridge message fee are paid separately.

**Time:** about 14 min from BTC, about 9 min to BTC · **You receive:** 817.64 USDT on Ethereum for 0.01 BTC (L2Pass RFQ) · **Gas:** Bitcoin network fee when you send BTC

## Routes for BTC and USDT

| Route | Estimated time | Output | Notes |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| L2Pass RFQ | About 13.5 to 14 minutes from BTC, about 9 minutes to BTC | Variable, with a minimum received | Solvers and market makers compete to fill your swap. Deposit address. L2Pass fee up to 0.2%. |
| L2Pass Private | Same as L2Pass RFQ | Variable, with a minimum received | Confidential execution. Deposit address. L2Pass fee up to 0.3% (replacing the standard fee, not added to it). |

No other routes were offered for these pairs in those test quotes. The quote lists any route that can fill the pair.

## How to swap BTC to USDT

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open L2Pass Swap">
    Go to [swap.l2pass.com](https://swap.l2pass.com/) and fill in four fields:

    - **From:** BTC on Bitcoin.
    - **To:** USDT on Tron or Ethereum.
    - **Amount:** how much BTC you want to swap.
    - **Receiving address:** the Tron or Ethereum address that will get the USDT.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Compare the routes">
    Check the expected output, minimum received, estimated time and fees on the L2Pass RFQ and L2Pass Private cards. Default slippage is 0.05% (editable).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set a refund address">
    Set the refund address to a Bitcoin address you control. If the swap cannot complete, your BTC is returned there.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Send BTC to the deposit address">
    Send the amount shown in the quote to the one-time Bitcoin deposit address from any Bitcoin wallet or an exchange, by address or QR code. If you send from an exchange, check that the amount that arrives matches the quote, since some exchanges deduct their withdrawal fee from it.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Track the swap">
    Follow the status in **Recent swaps**: *Awaiting deposit*, *Deposit confirmed*, then *Complete*. The deposit is confirmed after Bitcoin confirms your transaction.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To swap USDT to BTC, choose USDT on Tron or Ethereum as the source and BTC as the destination, set a Bitcoin receiving address, set a refund address on your USDT network and send the USDT to the deposit address in the same way.

## Before you send

- **Bitcoin fees.** You pay the Bitcoin network fee when you send BTC. When you receive BTC, the Bitcoin network fee is deducted from the amount shown.
- **Timing.** Bitcoin blocks come about every 10 minutes, so swaps from or to BTC take longer than stablecoin transfers between other chains.
- **Minimum amount.** Some routes have a minimum swap amount. If your amount is below it, the route is listed as unavailable with the minimum shown.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need to wrap BTC to swap it to USDT?">
    No. You send native BTC on Bitcoin to the deposit address, and USDT arrives on Tron or Ethereum.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How much does it cost to swap BTC to USDT?">
    The L2Pass fee is up to 0.2% (up to 0.3% on private swaps). In test quotes, L2Pass RFQ showed a 0% L2Pass fee, a 0.2% NEAR Intents provider fee and a destination network fee. The amount shown already includes all of them. You also pay the Bitcoin network fee when you send.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How long does a BTC to USDT swap take?">
    About 13.5 to 14 minutes in test quotes, and about 9 minutes from USDT to BTC. Route cards show the current estimate.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need an account?">
    No. You do not need an account or KYC for regular use, and you can pay from any wallet or exchange.
  </Accordion>
