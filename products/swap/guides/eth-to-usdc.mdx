You can swap ETH to USDC or USDT on another chain in one step with [L2Pass Swap](https://swap.l2pass.com/), for example ETH on Ethereum to USDC on Base, or ETH on Arbitrum to USDT on Tron. In test quotes, L2Pass RFQ, Relay, LI.FI and L2Pass Private were offered between EVM chains, with estimated times from about 1 second to under a minute, and L2Pass RFQ and Private were offered to Tron. The L2Pass fee on ETH swaps is up to 0.2% (up to 0.3% on private swaps), and the quote shows what you will receive after fees. Only source-chain gas and any bridge message fee are paid separately.

**Time:** about 49 sec · **You receive:** 2,387.76 USDC on Base for 1 ETH on Ethereum (L2Pass RFQ) · **Gas:** ETH on the source chain only

<Note>Figures are from test quotes on October 19, 2026. Your live quote shows current numbers.</Note>

| Route | Estimated time | Output | Payment | L2Pass fee in test quotes |

## How to swap ETH to USDC on another chain

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open L2Pass Swap">
    Go to [swap.l2pass.com](https://swap.l2pass.com/) and fill in four fields:

    - **From:** ETH on the network you hold it on.
    - **To:** USDC or USDT on the destination network.
    - **Amount:** how much ETH you want to swap.
    - **Receiving address:** the address on the destination network that will get the stablecoins.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Compare the routes">
    Each route card shows the expected output, minimum received, estimated time and fees. ETH prices move, so check the minimum received. Default slippage is 0.05% (editable).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set a refund address">
    For L2Pass RFQ and L2Pass Private, set the refund address to a wallet you control on the source chain. If the swap cannot complete, your ETH is returned there.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pay by wallet or deposit address">
    How you pay depends on the route you picked.

    <Tabs>
      <Tab title="Wallet routes (Relay, LI.FI)">
        Sign the transaction in your wallet.
      </Tab>
      <Tab title="Deposit address (L2Pass RFQ, L2Pass Private)">
        Send ETH to the one-time deposit address from your connected wallet, any wallet or an exchange, by address or QR code. If you send from an exchange, check that the amount that arrives matches the quote, since some exchanges deduct their withdrawal fee from it.
      </Tab>
    </Tabs>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Track the swap">
    For L2Pass RFQ and L2Pass Private, follow the status in **Recent swaps**: *Awaiting deposit*, *Deposit confirmed*, then *Complete*.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Before you send

- **Keep some ETH for gas.** You pay source gas in ETH, so do not swap your whole balance on a wallet route. On a deposit-address route, the network fee for your transfer comes out of your wallet too.
- **Price moves.** ETH is volatile. The minimum received protects you: if the price moves beyond your slippage tolerance, the swap does not execute.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I swap ETH to USDC and bridge it in one step?">
    Yes. Choose ETH on the source network and USDC on the destination network. The route swaps and moves it in one swap.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How much does it cost to swap ETH to USDC across chains?">
    The L2Pass fee on ETH swaps is up to 0.2% (up to 0.3% on private swaps). In test quotes, it was 0% on L2Pass RFQ and 0.1% on Relay and LI.FI. Providers and networks charge their own fees, and the amount shown already includes them.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I swap ETH on Arbitrum to USDT on Tron?">
    Yes. In test quotes, L2Pass RFQ delivered 2,388.75 USDT on Tron for 1 ETH on Arbitrum, in about 80 seconds.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Which route is fastest?">
    Relay showed about 1 to 2 seconds between EVM chains in test quotes. L2Pass RFQ took about 34 to 49 seconds but delivered as much or more. Compare the route cards for your amount.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
