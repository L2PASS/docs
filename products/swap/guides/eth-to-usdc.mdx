You can swap ETH to USDC or USDT on another chain in one step with [L2Pass Swap](https://swap.l2pass.com/), for example ETH on Ethereum to USDC on Base, or ETH on Arbitrum to USDT on Tron. In test quotes on October 19, 2026, L2Pass RFQ, Relay, LI.FI and L2Pass Private were offered between EVM chains, with estimated times from about 1 second to under a minute, and L2Pass RFQ and Private were offered to Tron. The L2Pass fee on ETH swaps is up to 0.2% (up to 0.3% on private swaps), and every fee is already included in the amount shown in the quote.

## How to swap ETH to USDC on another chain

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open L2Pass Swap">
    Go to [swap.l2pass.com](https://swap.l2pass.com/), choose ETH on the network you hold it on as the source and USDC or USDT on the destination network, then enter the amount and set the receiving address.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Compare the routes">
    Each route card shows the expected output, minimum received, estimated time and fees. ETH prices move, so check the minimum received. Default slippage is 0.05% (editable).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set a refund address">
    For L2Pass RFQ and L2Pass Private, set the refund address to a wallet you control on the source chain. If the swap cannot complete, your ETH is returned there.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pay by wallet or deposit address">
    On Relay and LI.FI, sign the transaction in your wallet. On L2Pass RFQ and L2Pass Private, send ETH to the one-time deposit address from your connected wallet, any wallet or an exchange, by address or QR code.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Track the swap">
    For L2Pass RFQ and L2Pass Private, follow the status in **Recent swaps**: *Awaiting deposit*, *Deposit confirmed*, then *Complete*.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Things to know

- **Keep some ETH for gas.** You pay source gas in ETH, so do not swap your whole balance on a wallet route. On a deposit-address route, the network fee for your transfer comes out of your wallet too.
- **Price moves.** ETH is volatile. The minimum received protects you: if the price moves beyond your slippage tolerance, the swap does not execute.
- **Refunds.** On-chain routes revert (gas is not refundable). Deposit-address routes return your deposit to the refund address; provider and network fees may be deducted.
- **No partial fills.** A swap completes in full or does not execute.
- **Compliance checks.** No account or KYC is needed for regular use, though a provider may request extra checks for a specific transaction. If a provider flags a swap as high risk under AML or sanctions rules, it decides whether to complete, refund or hold it, and a refund is not guaranteed.
