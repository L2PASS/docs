You can bridge USDC to Solana from Ethereum or Base, and swap USDT on Tron to USDC on Solana, with [L2Pass Swap](https://swap.l2pass.com/). In test quotes, these pairs were offered by L2Pass RFQ and L2Pass Private, both run on [NEAR Intents](https://near.org/intents), with estimated times of about 30 seconds to 1.5 minutes. You pay by sending tokens to a one-time deposit address from your connected wallet, any wallet or an exchange. There is a 0% L2Pass fee on stablecoin swaps (up to 0.3% on private swaps), and the quote shows what you will receive after fees before you confirm. Only source-chain gas and any bridge message fee are paid separately.

## Routes to Solana

Only L2Pass RFQ and L2Pass Private reached Solana in test quotes, from Base, Ethereum and Tron.

| Route | Estimated time | Output | Notes |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| L2Pass RFQ | About 32 seconds from Base, 42 seconds from Ethereum, 77 seconds from Tron | Variable, with a minimum received | Solvers and market makers compete to fill your swap. Deposit address. |
| L2Pass Private | Same as L2Pass RFQ | Variable, with a minimum received | Confidential execution. Deposit address. L2Pass fee up to 0.3%. |

No other routes, including Circle CCTP, were offered for these pairs in those test quotes. The quote lists any route that can fill your pair.

## How to bridge USDC to Solana

Nothing leaves your wallet until you pay, so you can compare every route first.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open L2Pass Swap">
    Go to [swap.l2pass.com](https://swap.l2pass.com/) and fill in four fields:

    - **From:** USDC on Ethereum or Base, or USDT on Tron.
    - **To:** USDC on Solana.
    - **Amount:** how much you want to send.
    - **Receiving address:** the Solana address that will get the USDC.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Compare the routes">
    Check the expected output, minimum received, estimated time and fees on the L2Pass RFQ and L2Pass Private cards. Default slippage is 0.05% (editable).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set a refund address">
    Set the refund address to a wallet you control on the source chain. If the swap cannot complete, the provider sends your deposit back there.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pay to the deposit address">
    Send the amount shown in the quote to the one-time deposit address from your connected wallet, any wallet or an exchange, by address or QR code. If you send from an exchange, check that the amount that arrives matches the quote.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Track the swap">
    Follow the status in **Recent swaps**: *Awaiting deposit*, *Deposit confirmed*, then *Complete*.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Before you send

You need gas only on the source chain and no SOL to receive.

- **Gas on the source chain only.** You pay gas in ETH on Ethereum or Base, or in TRX on Tron. The Solana network fee is deducted from the amount shown, so you do not need SOL to receive. You need SOL later to move or use the USDC on Solana.
- **USDT to USDC in one swap.** From Tron, you can change the stablecoin and the network at once. It is still a stablecoin swap, with a 0% L2Pass fee on L2Pass RFQ.
