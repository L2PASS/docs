You can bridge USDT from Tron (TRC-20) to BNB Chain (BEP-20) with [L2Pass Swap](https://swap.l2pass.com/), a non-custodial interface that shows every route for the pair side by side. In test quotes, it offered two routes for this pair, L2Pass RFQ and L2Pass Private, each with an estimated time of about 1.5 minutes. There is a 0% L2Pass fee on stablecoin swaps (up to 0.3% on private swaps), and the quote shows what you will receive after fees before you confirm. Only source-chain gas and any bridge message fee are paid separately.

**Time:** about 1.5 min · **You receive:** 1,008.88 USDT on 1,010 USDT (L2Pass RFQ) · **Minimum:** about 1,000 USD on L2Pass Private · **Gas:** TRX on Tron only

<Note>Figures are from test quotes on October 10, 2026. Your live quote shows current numbers.</Note>

## Routes for USDT from Tron to BNB Chain

L2Pass RFQ and L2Pass Private both run on [NEAR Intents](https://near.org/intents), where solvers and market makers compete to fill your swap. Private executes it in a confidential environment.

| Route | Estimated time | Output | Notes |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| L2Pass RFQ | About 1.5 minutes | Variable, with a minimum received | Pay to a one-time deposit address. |
| L2Pass Private | About 1.5 minutes | Variable, with a minimum received | Deposit address. L2Pass fee up to 0.3%. Minimum about 1,000 USD. |

The native USDT0 bridge and aggregator routes (LI.FI, LI.FI Intents, Relay, Aori, OKX DEX aggregator) were not offered for this pair in those test quotes. The quote lists any route that can fill the pair.

Test quotes for 1,010 USDT showed these amounts received: 1,008.88 USDT on L2Pass RFQ and 1,007.26 USDT on L2Pass Private. The live quote shows current numbers for your amount.

## How to bridge USDT from Tron to BNB Chain

Nothing leaves your wallet until you pay, so you can compare every route first.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open L2Pass Swap">
    Go to [swap.l2pass.com](https://swap.l2pass.com/) and fill in four fields:

    - **From:** USDT on Tron.
    - **To:** USDT on BNB Chain.
    - **Amount:** how much USDT you want to send.
    - **Receiving address:** the BNB Chain address that will get the USDT.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Compare the routes">
    Each route card shows the expected output, minimum received, estimated time and fees. Default slippage is 0.05% (editable) and does not apply to fixed-output routes such as USDT0.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set a refund address">
    For L2Pass RFQ and L2Pass Private, set the refund address to a Tron wallet you control. If the swap cannot complete, the provider sends your deposit back there.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pay to the deposit address">
    Send USDT to the one-time deposit address from your connected wallet, any Tron wallet or an exchange, by address or QR code. If you send from an exchange, check that the amount that arrives matches the quote, since some exchanges deduct their withdrawal fee from it.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Track the swap in Recent swaps">
    Follow the status in **Recent swaps**: *Awaiting deposit*, *Deposit confirmed*, then *Complete*.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Before you send

You need TRX on Tron for gas and no BNB to receive, and L2Pass Private needs at least about 1,000 USD.

- **Gas on Tron only.** Sending TRC-20 USDT costs TRX for energy and bandwidth. Any network fee on the destination chain is deducted from the amount shown, so you do not need gas there.
- **Minimum amount.** In test quotes, L2Pass Private needed at least 1,000 USD for this pair. If your amount is below a route's minimum, the route is listed as unavailable with the minimum shown.
