You can bridge USDT from Tron (TRC-20) to Solana (SPL) with [L2Pass Swap](https://swap.l2pass.com/), a non-custodial interface that shows every route for the pair side by side. In test quotes, it offered two routes for this pair, L2Pass RFQ and L2Pass Private, each with an estimated time of about 80 seconds. There is a 0% L2Pass fee on stablecoin swaps (up to 0.3% on private swaps), and the quote shows what you will receive after fees before you confirm. Only source-chain gas and any bridge message fee are paid separately.

**Time:** about 80 sec · **You receive:** 999.87 USDT on 1,000 USDT (L2Pass RFQ) · **Gas:** TRX on Tron only

<Note>Figures are from test quotes on October 10, 2026. Your live quote shows current numbers.</Note>

## Routes for USDT from Tron to Solana

L2Pass RFQ and L2Pass Private both run on [NEAR Intents](https://near.org/intents), where solvers and market makers compete to fill your swap. Private executes it in a confidential environment.

| Route | Estimated time | Output | Notes |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| L2Pass RFQ | About 80 seconds | Variable, with a minimum received | Pay to a one-time deposit address. |
| L2Pass Private | About 80 seconds | Variable, with a minimum received | Deposit address. L2Pass fee up to 0.3%. |

The native USDT0 bridge and aggregator routes (LI.FI, LI.FI Intents, Relay, Aori, OKX DEX aggregator) were not offered for this pair in those test quotes. The quote lists any route that can fill the pair.

Test quotes for 1,000 USDT showed these amounts received: 999.87 USDT on L2Pass RFQ and 997.29 USDT on L2Pass Private. The live quote shows current numbers for your amount.

## How to bridge USDT from Tron to Solana

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open L2Pass Swap">
    Go to [swap.l2pass.com](https://swap.l2pass.com/), choose USDT on Tron as the source and USDT on Solana as the destination, then enter the amount and set the Solana address that will receive the USDT.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Compare the routes">
    Each route card shows the expected output, minimum received, estimated time and fees. Default slippage is 0.05% (editable) and does not apply to fixed-output routes such as USDT0.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set a refund address">
    For L2Pass RFQ and L2Pass Private, set the refund address to a Tron wallet you control. If the swap cannot complete, your deposit is returned there.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pay to the deposit address">
    Send USDT to the one-time deposit address from your connected wallet, any Tron wallet or an exchange, by address or QR code. If you send from an exchange, check that the amount that arrives matches the quote, since some exchanges deduct their withdrawal fee from it.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Track the swap in Recent swaps">
    Follow the status in **Recent swaps**: *Awaiting deposit*, *Deposit confirmed*, then *Complete*.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Before you send

- **Gas on Tron only.** Sending TRC-20 USDT costs TRX for energy and bandwidth. Any network fee on the destination chain is deducted from the amount shown, so you do not need gas there.
- **Minimum amount.** Some routes have a minimum swap amount that varies by pair. If your amount is below it, the route is listed as unavailable with the minimum shown.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What will I receive on Solana?">
    USDT as an SPL token, the token standard on Solana. Use a Solana address as the destination; Tron and 0x addresses will not work.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need SOL to receive USDT on Solana?">
    No. You pay gas only on Tron, in TRX, and any network fee on the destination chain is already deducted from the amount shown. You need a small amount of SOL later to move or use the USDT.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I receive USDC or SOL on Solana instead?">
    Yes, if a route can fill the pair: pick another destination token and the quote updates. There is a 0% L2Pass fee on stablecoin swaps, such as USDT to USDC, and up to 0.2% on other assets, such as SOL.
  </Accordion>
