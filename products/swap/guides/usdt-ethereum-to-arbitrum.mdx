You can bridge USDT from Ethereum to Arbitrum with [L2Pass Swap](https://swap.l2pass.com/). On Arbitrum, USDT arrives as USDT0, the omnichain version of USDT. In test quotes on October 19, 2026, six routes were offered for this pair: the native USDT0 bridge, L2Pass RFQ, LI.FI Intents, Relay, L2Pass Private and LI.FI, with estimated times from about 1 second to 1.5 minutes. The top routes delivered within 0.3 USDT of each other for 1,000 USDT. There is a 0% L2Pass fee on stablecoin swaps (up to 0.3% on private swaps), and every fee is already included in the amount you will receive, shown in the quote before you confirm.

## How to bridge USDT from Ethereum to Arbitrum

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open L2Pass Swap">
    Go to [swap.l2pass.com](https://swap.l2pass.com/), choose USDT on Ethereum as the source and USDT0 on Arbitrum as the destination, then enter the amount and set the Arbitrum address that will receive the USDT0.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Compare the routes">
    Each route card shows the expected output, minimum received, estimated time and fees. If speed matters, Relay settled in about 1 second in test quotes; if the amount matters most, compare USDT0 and L2Pass RFQ.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set a refund address">
    For L2Pass RFQ and L2Pass Private, set the refund address to an Ethereum wallet you control. If the swap cannot complete, your deposit is returned there.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pay by wallet or deposit address">
    On USDT0, Relay and LI.FI, sign the transaction in your Ethereum wallet. On L2Pass RFQ and L2Pass Private, send USDT to the one-time deposit address from your connected wallet, any wallet or an exchange.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Track the swap">
    For L2Pass RFQ and L2Pass Private, follow the status in **Recent swaps**. A USDT0 transfer is a LayerZero message, so you can follow it on [LayerZero Scan](https://layerzeroscan.com/) with your Ethereum transaction hash.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Things to know

- **Gas on Ethereum only.** You pay gas in ETH on Ethereum. The Arbitrum network fee is deducted from the amount shown, so you do not need ETH on Arbitrum to receive.
- **USDT0 on Arbitrum.** The USDT that L2Pass Swap delivers on Arbitrum is USDT0.
- **Refunds.** On-chain routes revert (gas is not refundable). Deposit-address routes return your deposit to the refund address; provider and network fees may be deducted.
- **No partial fills.** A swap completes in full or does not execute.
- **Compliance checks.** No account or KYC is needed for regular use, though a provider may request extra checks for a specific transaction. If a provider flags a swap as high risk under AML or sanctions rules, it decides whether to complete, refund or hold it, and a refund is not guaranteed.
