You can bridge USDT from Tron (TRC-20) to TON, where USDT is a jetton, with [L2Pass Swap](https://swap.l2pass.com/), a non-custodial interface that shows every route for the pair side by side. In test quotes, it offered two routes for this pair, L2Pass RFQ and L2Pass Private, each with an estimated time of just over 2 minutes. There is a 0% L2Pass fee on stablecoin swaps (up to 0.3% on private swaps), and the quote shows what you will receive after fees before you confirm. Only source-chain gas and any bridge message fee are paid separately.

## Routes for USDT from Tron to TON

L2Pass RFQ and L2Pass Private both run on [NEAR Intents](https://near.org/intents), where solvers and market makers compete to fill your swap. Private executes it in a confidential environment.

| Route | Estimated time | Output | Notes |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| L2Pass RFQ | Just over 2 minutes | Variable, with a minimum received | Pay to a one-time deposit address. |
| L2Pass Private | Just over 2 minutes | Variable, with a minimum received | Deposit address. L2Pass fee up to 0.3%. |

The native USDT0 bridge and aggregator routes (LI.FI, LI.FI Intents, Relay, Aori, OKX DEX aggregator) were not offered for this pair in those test quotes. The quote lists any route that can fill the pair.

Test quotes for 1,000 USDT showed these amounts received: 998.84 USDT on L2Pass RFQ and 997.24 USDT on L2Pass Private. The live quote shows current numbers for your amount.

## How to bridge USDT from Tron to TON

Nothing leaves your wallet until you pay, so you can compare every route first.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open L2Pass Swap">
    Go to [swap.l2pass.com](https://swap.l2pass.com/) and fill in four fields:

    - **From:** USDT on Tron.
    - **To:** USDT on TON.
    - **Amount:** how much USDT you want to send.
    - **Receiving address:** the TON address that will get the USDT.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Compare the routes">
    Each route card shows the expected output, minimum received, estimated time and fees. Default slippage is 0.05% (editable) and does not apply to fixed-output routes such as USDT0.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set a refund address">
    For L2Pass RFQ and L2Pass Private, set the refund address to a Tron wallet you control. If the swap cannot complete, the provider sends your deposit back there.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pay to the deposit address">
    Send USDT to the one-time deposit address from your connected wallet, any Tron wallet or an exchange, by address or QR code. If you send from an exchange, check that the amount that arrives matches the quote, since some exchanges deduct their withdrawal fee from it.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Track the swap in Recent swaps">
    Follow the status in **Recent swaps**: *Awaiting deposit*, *Deposit confirmed*, then *Complete*.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Before you send

You need TRX on Tron for gas and no Toncoin to receive.

- **Gas on Tron only.** Sending TRC-20 USDT costs TRX for energy and bandwidth. Any network fee on the destination chain is deducted from the amount shown, so you do not need gas there.
- **Minimum amount.** Some routes have a minimum swap amount that varies by pair. If your amount is below it, the route is listed as unavailable with the minimum shown.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What will I receive on TON?">
    USDT as a jetton, the token standard on TON. Use a TON wallet address as the destination.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is the USDT0 bridge available from Tron to TON?">
    Not in test quotes: the USDT0 native bridge was not offered for this pair. Use L2Pass RFQ or L2Pass Private, which both showed an estimated time of just over 2 minutes. L2Pass Swap lists USDT0 whenever it can fill the pair.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I send USDT from Tron to an exchange account on TON?">
    Exchange deposit addresses on TON often require a memo (comment) to credit your account. To be safe, send the USDT to a TON wallet you control, then move it to the exchange.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need Toncoin to receive USDT on TON?">
    No. You pay gas only on Tron, in TRX, and any network fee on the destination chain is already deducted from the amount shown. You need a small amount of Toncoin (TON) later to move or use the USDT.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
