You can bridge native USDC between Ethereum, Arbitrum and Base with Circle CCTP in [L2Pass Swap](https://swap.l2pass.com/), shown as the USDC native bridge. It has two modes: Fast, which took about 40 to 50 seconds in test quotes on October 19, 2026, and Standard, which took about 19.5 minutes and charged a lower network fee. Both deliver a fixed amount, and L2Pass shows them next to other routes such as Relay, L2Pass RFQ and LI.FI so you can compare. There is a 0% L2Pass fee on stablecoin swaps, and every fee is already included in the amount you will receive, shown in the quote before you confirm.

## What Circle CCTP is

CCTP (Cross-Chain Transfer Protocol) is Circle's own way of moving USDC between chains. USDC is burned on the source chain and the same amount of native USDC is minted on the destination chain, so you receive the USDC that Circle issues there.

| Mode | How it works | Estimated time in test quotes | Network fee for 1,000 USDC |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| Fast | Circle confirms the transfer before the source chain reaches finality | About 38 seconds from Arbitrum, about 50 seconds from Ethereum | 0.19 USDC from Arbitrum, 0.15 USDC from Ethereum |
| Standard | Waits for block finality on the source chain | About 19.5 minutes | 0.05 USDC |

## How to bridge USDC with CCTP

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open L2Pass Swap">
    Go to [swap.l2pass.com](https://swap.l2pass.com/), choose USDC on the source network (for example Arbitrum) and USDC on the destination network (for example Base), then enter the amount and set the address that will receive the USDC.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pick Fast or Standard">
    Find the USDC native bridge cards. Choose Fast if you want the USDC in under a minute, or Standard if you can wait about 20 minutes for a lower network fee. Compare them with the other route cards.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Approve and sign">
    Sign the transaction in your wallet on the source network. If this is your first USDC transfer through the route, your wallet may ask for a token approval first.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Track the transfer">
    If the app asks for a final step on the destination network, follow it; an unfinished transfer can be resumed from the app later. You can check that the USDC has arrived by looking up the receiving address on a block explorer for the destination network, such as Basescan for Base.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Things to know

- **Gas on the source chain.** You pay gas in ETH on Ethereum, Arbitrum or Base. The CCTP network fee is deducted from the amount shown, so you do not need ETH on the destination chain to receive.
- **Fixed output.** CCTP delivers the exact amount shown, so slippage does not apply.
- **Wallet only.** CCTP needs a transaction signed in your wallet. To pay from an exchange, use L2Pass RFQ, which takes a deposit to a one-time address.
- **Refunds.** On-chain routes revert if they fail, and gas is not refundable.
- **No partial fills.** A transfer completes in full or does not execute.
