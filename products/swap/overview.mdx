[L2Pass Swap](https://swap.l2pass.com/) moves tokens between blockchains, with a focus on stablecoins. For every swap it asks several independent routing providers for a quote, shows them side by side sorted by what you receive, and lets you execute the one you pick with a single confirmation.

L2Pass never takes custody of your tokens. Third-party bridges, solver networks and liquidity sources execute each swap on their own infrastructure.

## Supported Networks and Assets

L2Pass Swap currently supports **Ethereum, Bitcoin, Zcash, Tron, Solana, TON, BNB Chain, Arbitrum, Base, Polygon, Optimism, Avalanche, Plasma and HyperEVM**.

Stablecoins (USDT, USDT0, USDC, DAI and others) are the main focus, but you can also swap major assets such as ETH, BTC, SOL, ZEC, TRX, BNB, AVAX and POL. Available tokens, networks and routes can change at any time; the [swap interface](https://swap.l2pass.com/) always shows the current list.

## How Routing Works

When you request a quote, L2Pass asks several routing venues at once and shows every route that can fill your swap, sorted by what you will receive:

- **L2Pass RFQ by NEAR Intents:** Request-for-quote routing through the [NEAR Intents](https://near.org/intents) network, where solvers and market makers compete to fill your swap.
- **L2Pass Private:** The same solver network, executed in a confidential environment. See [Private Swaps](#private-swaps).
- **USDT Native Bridge:** Native USDT0 transfers over LayerZero, including from Tron.
- **USDC Native Bridge:** Circle CCTP V2 transfers of native USDC, in Standard and Fast modes.
- **Aggregators and intent networks:** LI.FI, LI.FI Intents, Relay, Aori and the OKX DEX aggregator.

Each route card shows the expected output, the minimum received, the estimated time and the fees for that route, so you can pick the one that suits you. Some routes have a minimum swap amount; if your amount is below it, the route is listed as unavailable with the minimum shown.

## How a Swap Is Executed

Depending on the route, a swap is executed in one of two ways:

- **Wallet transaction:** You sign a transaction in your wallet and the route's contracts execute the swap and bridge on-chain.
- **Deposit address:** For RFQ and Private routes, you send tokens to a one-time deposit address for your quote. You can send from your connected wallet, or transfer manually from any wallet or exchange using the address or QR code. If you send from an exchange, check that the amount that arrives matches the quote, since some exchanges deduct their withdrawal fee. The swap then moves through *Awaiting deposit*, *Deposit confirmed* and *Complete*, which you can follow in **Recent swaps**.

For a deposit-address route, you also set a refund address on the source chain. If the swap cannot complete, the provider sends your deposit back there.

## How long a swap takes

Most swaps settle in seconds to a few minutes, and each route card shows its estimate before you confirm.

- **Relay, Aori and CCTP Fast:** seconds to about a minute.
- **L2Pass RFQ and L2Pass Private:** 30 seconds to a few minutes, and longer with Bitcoin or Zcash, which wait for block confirmations.
- **USDT0:** a few minutes.
- **CCTP Standard:** up to 20 minutes, while it waits for block finality.

Network congestion or provider downtime can make a swap take longer than its estimate.

## Slippage and Price Impact

**Price impact** is the price shift caused by the trade size relative to the available liquidity. Comparing several venues helps keep it low.

**Slippage** is the difference between the quoted price and the final execution price.

- **Slippage tolerance:** The default tolerance is **0.05%**. You can change it from any route card.
- **Fixed output:** Some routes, such as native USDT0 and USDC bridges, deliver a fixed amount, so slippage does not apply.
- **Minimum received:** Each route card shows the minimum received, the worst-case amount within your tolerance. If the price moves beyond it, the swap does not execute: on-chain routes revert, and deposit-address routes return your deposit to your refund address.

## Fee Structure

The route card shows the actual fees for your swap before you confirm. The **estimated amount on the destination network already includes all fees** except source-chain gas and any bridge message fee, which you pay separately.

### L2Pass Fee

| Swap type | L2Pass fee |
| :-- | :-- |
| Stablecoin swaps (any route) | **0%** |
| Other assets | **up to 0.2%** |
| Private swaps, any asset (instead of 0% or 0.2%, not in addition) | **up to 0.3%** |

### Partner and Network Fees

Routing partners (bridges, solver networks, aggregators and liquidity providers) may charge their own fees. L2Pass does not control these fees; for the most up-to-date information, please check each provider's documentation.

Every blockchain transaction also requires a network (gas) fee paid in the native token, such as ETH, POL, BNB or TRX. Gas fees go to the network, not to L2Pass, and are not refundable.

## Private Swaps

Private swaps route your swap through a confidential execution environment instead of a public one, which limits what is visible on public blockchains. They are made possible by NEAR Intents, a third-party protocol.

- **Not anonymity:** Your public wallet address is still included in requests sent to providers.
- **Same screening:** Private swaps are screened exactly like public ones, at the quote and after the deposit.
- **Fee:** The L2Pass fee for a private swap is up to 0.3% (instead of 0% or 0.2%, not in addition). Like every fee, it is already included in the amount shown in the quote.

See the [Terms of Service](https://l2pass.com/terms-of-service) and [Privacy & AML Principles](https://l2pass.com/privacy-policy) for details.

## Full fill or full refund

A swap completes in full or does not execute, and a failed swap is refunded automatically.

- **No partial fills:** L2Pass does not use partial execution. A swap either completes in full or does not execute.
- **Refunds:** If a deposit-address swap cannot complete, for example because the price moved beyond your tolerance or the quote expired, the provider sends your deposit back to your refund address, minus any provider and network fees. A failed wallet transaction reverts, and the gas you paid is spent.
- **Compliance reviews:** Every swap is screened, with no KYC. On L2Pass RFQ and L2Pass Private, a flagged address gets no quote. A deposit flagged after it arrives is held for review instead of refunded, and you resolve it with a ticket to NEAR Intents support. See [How screening works](/products/swap/learn/failed-swaps-and-refunds#how-screening-works).
