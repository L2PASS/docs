We requested live quotes for the same two stablecoin transfers from [L2Pass Swap](https://swap.l2pass.com/) and from other bridges and exchange services, on October 19, 2026, with the same amounts for every provider. For 1,000 USDT from Tron to Ethereum, L2Pass RFQ by NEAR Intents returned 999.55 USDT, more than deBridge, Relay, THORChain, LI.FI, ChangeNOW and SideShift. For 1,000 USDC from Arbitrum to Base, L2Pass showed eight routes, and its best one (Circle CCTP, 999.95 USDC) matched or beat every other provider. The L2Pass fee is 0% on stablecoin swaps on every route.

## What the numbers show

- **L2Pass compares providers for you.** On Arbitrum to Base, one L2Pass quote returned eight routes, including Relay, LI.FI and Circle CCTP, so you see the best one without visiting each site.
- **Fastest is not cheapest.** Relay and deBridge took seconds. CCTP Standard paid the most but took about 19.5 minutes.
- **Instant exchanges cost more.** ChangeNOW and SideShift returned 12 to 36 USDT less on a 1,000 USDT transfer.
- **Tron support varies.** MAYAChain does not support Tron, and LI.FI routed through LayerSwap.
- **Totals matter more than headline fees.** deBridge adds a fixed fee in the source chain's native token on top of its quote, and USDT0 adds a LayerZero message fee, so compare what arrives after everything you pay.

## Which one to use

- **Lowest total cost from Tron:** L2Pass RFQ.
- **Speed in seconds between EVM chains:** Relay, directly or inside L2Pass at the same price.
- **Largest USDC amount when you can wait:** Circle CCTP Standard, about 19.5 minutes.
- **Paying from an exchange account:** a deposit-address route such as L2Pass RFQ, because wallet-only bridges need you to sign from your own wallet.
