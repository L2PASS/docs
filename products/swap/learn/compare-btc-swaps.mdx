We requested live quotes for native BTC to USDT, and back, from [L2Pass Swap](https://swap.l2pass.com/) and other cross-chain services, on October 19, 2026, with the same amounts for every provider. For 0.01 BTC to USDT, on Tron and on Ethereum, L2Pass RFQ by NEAR Intents returned the most USDT, and side by side in the web interfaces it beat the THORChain app on every pair, in regular and private mode. For 1,000 USDT on Tron to BTC, Relay returned the most BTC. The L2Pass fee on BTC swaps is up to 0.2%, included in every amount below.

## What the numbers show

- **L2Pass beat the THORChain app on every pair we checked in both interfaces,** in regular and private mode. Relay returned the most for USDT on Tron to BTC.
- **Tron delivery costs vary a lot.** THORChain charged a 3.36 USDT outbound fee to Tron, and L2Pass charged 1.71 USDT.
- **Instant exchanges cost more.** ChangeNOW and SideShift returned 4 to 24 USDT less on 0.01 BTC.
- **Bitcoin confirmations set the pace.** Every native BTC swap waits for Bitcoin blocks, so a 4-second relayer still takes minutes in total.
- **Private swaps cost more everywhere, but less on L2Pass.** L2Pass Private returned 8.55 USDT more than Houdini Swap in the THORChain app.

## Which one to use

- **BTC to USDT on Tron:** L2Pass RFQ returned the most and accepts BTC from any wallet or exchange.
- **BTC to USDT on Ethereum:** L2Pass RFQ returned the most of the providers we checked.
- **USDT to BTC:** Relay returned the most in our test. L2Pass shows Relay's routes where Relay supports the pair.
- **Private BTC swaps:** L2Pass Private.
