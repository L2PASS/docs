## Things to know

- **Privacy is not anonymity.** L2Pass Private limits what public blockchains show about how the swap is filled. Providers still receive your wallet address, and compliance screening applies.
- **Receiving address.** Use a transparent Zcash address unless the quote accepts yours, then shield the ZEC in your wallet.
- **Compliance checks.** If a provider flags a swap as high risk, it decides whether to complete, refund or hold it, and a refund is not guaranteed.
