A centralized exchange holds your funds in your account. To trade or withdraw, you usually need to verify your identity (KYC), and the exchange can pause a withdrawal or freeze an account while it reviews it, for example by asking for the source of funds. [L2Pass Swap](https://swap.l2pass.com/) works differently: it is non-custodial, needs no account and no KYC, and your tokens stay in your own wallet until you send them into a swap. Each swap is still screened for AML and sanctions risk, and a flagged deposit can be held for review.

## Side by side

The main difference is who holds your funds: you, or the exchange.

| | L2Pass Swap (non-custodial) | Centralized exchange |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| Account | Not needed | Required |
| Identity verification (KYC) | Not needed | Usually required to trade or withdraw |
| Who holds your funds | You, in your own wallet | The exchange, in your account |
| Who holds the keys | Only you | The exchange |
| Cross-chain transfer | One swap, from one network to another | Deposit, trade if needed, then withdraw on the new network |
| Fees shown | Every fee and the amount you will receive, before you confirm | Trading fee, spread and withdrawal fee, each on its own screen |
| If a transaction is flagged | A flagged address gets no quote; a flagged deposit is held for review of that one swap | The exchange can hold the withdrawal or restrict the account |
| Fiat on and off ramp | No | Usually yes |

## Instant exchanges sit in between

Instant exchanges such as FixedFloat, ChangeNOW or StealthEX need no account, but they take custody of your deposit until they send the swap. [Trocador](https://trocador.app), which compares them, rates each one by its terms, privacy policy and what it does when a user refuses verification, and notes that any exchange can still pause a swap and ask for KYC or AML checks. In our live quotes on October 19, 2026, they also returned less than L2Pass RFQ on most pairs. See [Compare: stablecoin transfers](/products/swap/learn/compare-stablecoin-transfers), [BTC swaps](/products/swap/learn/compare-btc-swaps) and [ZEC swaps](/products/swap/learn/compare-zec-swaps).

## When each one fits

**A non-custodial swap fits when you:**
- Already hold crypto and want to move it to another network or token.
- Want to keep custody of your funds between swaps.
- Do not want to open or verify an account for a one-off transfer.

**A centralized exchange fits when you:**
- Need to buy or sell crypto for fiat money.
- Want an order book or trading tools.
- Are fine with keeping funds on the exchange.

You can also combine them: withdraw from an exchange straight to the one-time deposit address of an L2Pass quote, and the tokens arrive on the network you chose. See [Swap from any wallet or exchange](/products/swap/learn/deposit-address-swaps).

## What non-custodial does not mean

Non-custodial means L2Pass never holds your funds. It does not mean anonymous or unscreened.

- **Not anonymous.** Swaps are public blockchain transactions, and providers receive your wallet address.
- **Not unscreened.** Every swap is screened for AML and sanctions risk. A deposit flagged after it arrives is held for review instead of refunded, and you resolve it with NEAR Intents support. See [How screening works](/products/swap/learn/failed-swaps-and-refunds#how-screening-works).
- **Not a place to store funds.** L2Pass does not keep a balance for you. Your funds stay in your wallet.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need KYC to use L2Pass Swap?">
    No. There is no account and no KYC. If a deposit is held for review, NEAR Intents support may ask where the funds came from.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can L2Pass freeze my funds?">
    L2Pass never holds your funds, so it has no balance to freeze. If NEAR Intents flags a deposit under AML or sanctions rules, that one swap is held for review until you resolve it with their support. Nothing else is affected.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is L2Pass a DEX?">
    L2Pass Swap is a non-custodial interface that compares routes from decentralized protocols and solver networks, such as NEAR Intents, Circle CCTP, USDT0, LI.FI and Relay. It does not run an order book or hold user funds.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I move crypto off an exchange without another KYC check?">
    Withdraw from your exchange straight to the one-time deposit address of an L2Pass quote. L2Pass needs no account or KYC, and the tokens arrive on the network you chose. Your exchange's own rules still apply to the withdrawal.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why do exchanges ask for proof of funds?">
    Exchanges are custodians and must follow the rules where they are licensed, which can include checking where deposits come from. Ask your exchange for its exact requirements.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
