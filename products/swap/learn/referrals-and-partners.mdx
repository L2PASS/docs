L2Pass offers individual terms for referrals, partners and high-volume traders. If you bring users to [L2Pass Swap](https://swap.l2pass.com/), build it into your wallet or app, or swap large volumes yourself, the team can share part of the L2Pass fee with you or reduce the L2Pass fee you pay, depending on your volume. Terms are agreed one by one, so there is no public rate card. To get started, write to the team through one of the channels below.

## What can be agreed

| Who you are | What can be agreed |
| :-- | :-- |
| Referrer, community or creator | A share of the L2Pass fee on swaps from users you bring |
| Wallet, app, exchange or merchant | A share of the L2Pass fee, and your own partner fee on top through the [Swap API](/products/swap/learn/swap-api) |
| Trader, desk or business with regular volume | A reduced L2Pass fee that depends on your monthly volume |

## What the terms apply to

Only the L2Pass fee can be shared or reduced. The L2Pass fee is 0% on stablecoin swaps, up to 0.2% on other assets and up to 0.3% on private swaps. Routing partners and blockchains charge their own fees, which L2Pass does not control. See [L2Pass Swap fees explained](/products/swap/learn/fees).

The amount shown in a quote already includes every fee, so users see the final amount before they confirm.

Include these details so the team can answer quickly:

- **Who you are:** your project, community or company, and a link to it.
- **What you want:** referrals, an integration or lower fees.
- **Expected volume:** your monthly swap volume, even as a rough range.
- **Assets and networks:** for example USDT on Tron or BTC.
- **How you would send users or volume:** a link, the Swap API or direct swaps.

## Things to know

- **Terms are individual.** There is no fixed percentage for referrals or volume discounts. The team agrees them with you after reviewing your volume and use case.
- **Stablecoin swaps are already at 0%.** A reduced L2Pass fee matters mostly for BTC, ETH, ZEC and other non-stable assets and for private swaps.
- **Official channels only.** Team members never message you first and never ask for your private keys or seed phrase. See [Support](/resources/support).

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is there a referral program?">
    Yes. L2Pass can share part of the L2Pass fee with referrers and partners. The terms are agreed individually, so contact the team through Telegram or email.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I get a lower fee for high volume?">
    Yes. The L2Pass fee can be reduced depending on your volume. Send the team your expected monthly volume, assets and networks.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I earn a fee by integrating L2Pass into my app?">
    Yes. The [Swap API](/products/swap/learn/swap-api) lets you add your own partner fee, returned in every quote next to the platform fee. You can also ask the team to share part of the L2Pass fee.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Which fees can be reduced?">
    Only the L2Pass fee. Fees charged by routing partners and blockchains are outside L2Pass control, and the quote already includes them.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
