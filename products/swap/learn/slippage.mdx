In [L2Pass Swap](https://swap.l2pass.com/), every route card shows an expected output and a minimum received. The expected output is the estimated amount you will receive, already including all fees. The minimum received is the worst case within your slippage tolerance, which is 0.05% by default and can be changed on any route card. If the price moves beyond it, the swap does not execute. Fixed-output routes, such as Circle CCTP, USDT0, Aori and LI.FI Intents, deliver the exact amount shown, so slippage does not apply to them.

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
| :-- | :-- |
| Expected output | The estimated amount on the destination network, after all fees |
| Minimum received | The least you can receive within your slippage tolerance |
| Slippage | The difference between the quoted price and the final execution price, caused by market movement |
| Slippage tolerance | How much slippage you accept. Default 0.05% in L2Pass Swap |
| Price impact | The change in price caused by the size of your trade relative to available liquidity |
| Fixed output | A route that delivers exactly the amount shown, with no slippage |

## How to set slippage

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the route card">
    Request a quote on [swap.l2pass.com](https://swap.l2pass.com/) and open the route you want.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Change the tolerance">
    Edit the slippage tolerance on the route card. A higher tolerance makes a volatile swap more likely to execute but lowers the minimum received.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Check the minimum received">
    Confirm only if you are fine with receiving the minimum received in the worst case.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How to choose a tolerance

- **Stablecoin to stablecoin.** Prices barely move, so the default 0.05% is usually enough.
- **Volatile assets, such as ETH or BTC.** Prices can move while a swap settles, especially on slower routes. If a swap keeps failing on price, a slightly higher tolerance can help.
- **Fixed-output routes.** Tolerance does not matter: you get the exact amount shown.

## Things to know

- **No partial fills.** If the price moves beyond your tolerance, the swap does not execute at all.
- **Refunds.** A failed on-chain route reverts (gas is not refundable). A failed deposit-address route returns your deposit to the refund address; provider and network fees may be deducted.
- **Slippage is not a fee.** It is a limit on price movement. Fees are shown separately on the route card and are already included in the expected output.
